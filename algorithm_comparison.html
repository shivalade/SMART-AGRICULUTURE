📶 Flooding Algorithm (Existing) Existing

Traditional flooding routing sends packets to all neighboring nodes, ensuring delivery but at the cost of high bandwidth and energy consumption.

Algorithm Characteristics

  • Broadcasts packets to all neighbor nodes
  • Simple implementation and no routing table required
  • Guaranteed delivery in connected networks
  • High network congestion and packet duplication
  • Significant energy consumption on all nodes
  • Limited scalability with network size
